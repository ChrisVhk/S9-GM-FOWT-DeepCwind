# Références bibliographiques (documents publics)

Fiches détaillées dans ce dossier. Toutes ces sources sont publiques (NREL, DOE/OSTI, IOPscience
en accès ouvert) — aucune n'est reproduite ici au-delà d'un résumé et de citations courtes.

1. Robertson, A. et al. (2014). *Definition of the Semisubmersible Floating System for Phase II
   of OC4*. NREL/TP-5000-60601. DOI: 10.2172/1155123.
2. Robertson, A.; Jonkman, J.; Wendt, F.; Goupee, A.; Dagher, H. *Definition of the OC5 DeepCwind
   Semisubmersible Floating System*, v1.5. Wind Data Hub, `oc5.phase2`.
3. Robertson, A.N. et al. (2017). *OC5 Project Phase II: Validation of Global Loads of the
   DeepCwind Floating Semisubmersible Wind Turbine*. Energy Procedia, 137.
   DOI: 10.1016/j.egypro.2017.10.333.
4. Wendt, F.F.; Robertson, A.N.; Jonkman, J.M. (2019). *FAST Model Calibration and Validation of
   the OC5-DeepCwind Floating Offshore Wind System Against Wave Tank Test Data*. Int. J. Offshore
   Polar Eng., 29(1). DOI: 10.17736/ijope.2019.jc729.
5. Robertson, A.; Bachynski, E.E.; Gueydon, S.; Wendt, F.; Schünemann, P. (2019). *Total
   Experimental Uncertainty in Hydrodynamic Testing of a Semisubmersible Wind Turbine, Considering
   Numerical Propagation of Systematic Uncertainty*. Ocean Engineering.
6. Robertson, A.N. et al. (2020). *OC6 Phase I: Investigating the underprediction of low-frequency
   hydrodynamic loads and responses of a floating wind turbine*. J. Phys. Conf. Ser. 1618, 032033.
   DOI: 10.1088/1742-6596/1618/3/032033 (accès ouvert).
7. Wang, L.; Robertson, A.; Jonkman, J. et al. *OC6 Phase Ib: Validation of the CFD Predictions of
   Difference-Frequency Wave Excitation on a FOWT Semisubmersible*. Ocean Engineering.
